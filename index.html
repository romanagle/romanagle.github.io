About Me

I'm a junior at UC Berkeley pursuing a dual degree in Molecular & Cellular Biology and Data Science, with a domain emphasis in Biochemistry, Biophysics, and Structural Biology.

My research sits at the intersection of machine learning and molecular biology — specifically, building interpretable computational methods to understand how RNA sequence encodes function. I'm passionate about using deep learning to ask biological questions that were previously intractable.

I currently work in the Cate Lab at the Innovative Genomics Institute and spent the summer of 2025 as a URP student at Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory in the Koo Lab.

Research & Projects

Cold Spring Harbor Lab · Koo Lab

Combinatorial Mutagenesis via Machine-Guided Framework

Selected as 1 of 17 students in the 2025 URP cohort. Developed a machine-guided framework for combinatorial mutagenesis to study RNA sequence–function relationships, integrating deep learning models (ResidualBind) and surrogate modeling for interpretability.

Oral Presentation — ABRCMS 2025
GitHub
IGI Cate Lab · NeurIPS 2025

RNASSTR: RNA Secondary Structure Prediction

Trained, fine-tuned, and evaluated RNA secondary structure prediction models on a large database compiled from structural alignments in Rfam via Infernal. Implemented tokenization, hyperparameter optimization, and testing pipelines on the Lawrencium GPU cluster.

Accepted — NeurIPS 2025 AI4Science Workshop
BioRxiv NeurIPS Poster
Honors Thesis · IGI Cate Lab

Decoding tRNA Charging Specificity via ML

Developing interpretable machine-learning methods to study tRNA identity elements and charging specificity using evolutionary sequence data. Applying RNA-FM embeddings, softmax classifiers, and HPC pipelines (Infernal, tRNAscan-SE) to identify class-specific features.

Undergraduate Honors Thesis — In Progress
IGI Cate Lab · Bioinformatics

GTDB Phylogenetic rRNA Visualizer

Built an interactive web application for visualizing rRNA phylogenies, integrating a custom Perl module (MOTree) with a Flask frontend. Implemented backend pipelines to parse Newick files and generate scalable SVG trees using Perl, Python, HTML, and JavaScript.

IGI Science Day Poster
IGI Cate Lab · Structural Biology

YbeY Ribosomal Enzyme Analysis

Collaborated directly with Prof. Cate on a bioinformatics-based exploration of YbeY, a nearly universal enzyme that forms iso-aspartate in ribosomal subunit protein backbone. Analyzed gene neighborhoods and visualized structures in ChimeraX and Coot.

C-GEM · NLP & Cheminformatics

C-GEM Data Discovery Pipeline

Created an algorithm to scan and extract synthetase and monomer names from scientific literature and populate the C-GEM database. Developed a chemical formula–to–SMILES converter and locally deployed a user interface. Presented at symposium.
